## Adding new screenshots

- Capture the app window only — do not include the desktop, taskbar, or other
  applications.
- Do not include personal data, account names, email addresses, or tokens.
- Keep the desktop gallery at a consistent size (currently 1231 × 840).
- Export as WebP (quality 85–90) and update the table above and the main README.
