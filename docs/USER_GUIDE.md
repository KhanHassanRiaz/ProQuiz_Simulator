# ProQuiz Simulator — User Guide

Welcome to **ProQuiz Simulator**! This guide explains every feature so you can
prepare for competitive exams effectively.

## The main tabs

The application is divided into three tabs for easy navigation:

1. **Home** — your dashboard. Resume your last study session (MCQ or
   subjective), view overall progress, and check for updates.
2. **MCQ Preparation** — every tool for practising multiple-choice questions.
3. **Subjective Study** — detailed notes and courses.

## Using the MCQ tab

This tab contains four tools for your preparation.

> **Important first step: select your course.**
> At the top of the MCQ tab you will see a course selector.
> Choose **1 Paper MCQ GK** for one-paper GK subjects, or **Special Education**
> before opening any Special Education subject.

### 1. Learn (study mode)

Read and memorise questions — the correct answer is always visible.

1. Open the **MCQ Preparation** tab and select your course.
2. Click **Learn**.
3. Choose a subject from the dropdown.
4. Pick **Learn All Questions** or **Learn by a Specific Range**.
5. Click **Load MCQs** to start studying.

### 2. Take a quiz

Practise MCQs in a quiz format.

1. Open the **MCQ Preparation** tab and select your course.
2. Click **Take a Quiz**.
3. Select your subject.
4. Set your options:
   - **Timer** — time limit in minutes (enter `0` for no timer).
   - **Smart mode** — incorrect answers are asked again at the end.
5. Click **Start Quiz**.

### 3. Paste & create instant quiz

Use your own list of MCQs to create a temporary quiz.

1. Open the **MCQ Preparation** tab.
2. Click **Paste & Create Instant Quiz**.
3. Paste your questions into the text box.
4. Click **Start Quiz**.

### 4. Mock exam generator

Simulates a full-length examination paper, such as the PPSC 100-marks paper.

1. Open the **MCQ Preparation** tab and select your course.
2. Click **Mock Exam Generator**.
3. Choose an option:
   - **Default PPSC settings** — a 100-MCQ paper with the standard subject
     distribution.
   - **Configure manually** — build a custom paper by selecting subjects and
     their percentages.

## Using the Subjective Study tab

This tab is for reading detailed study material.

### Example: the Special Education syllabus

1. Select the **Subjective Study** tab from the main menu.
2. Click **Syllabus for Special Education Teacher (VI)**.
3. Use the first dropdown to select a **subject** (for example,
   *Administration and management*).
4. Use the second dropdown to select a **topic** from that subject.
5. Choose whether to read the **whole subject** or a **specific page range**.
6. Click **Load Content** to start reading.

### Reading in Roman Urdu

After the content loads, buttons appear below the subject heading. Click
**Show Roman Urdu** to view the translation.

### Example: the Beginners English course

1. Select the **Subjective Study** tab.
2. Click **Beginners English Learning Course**.
3. Select the topic you want to learn.
4. Click **Load Content** and start reading.

## Other features

- **Bookmarks** — save your position in both MCQ and subjective study modes.
- **Theme toggle** — use the icon in the top-right corner to switch between
  light and dark mode.
